# ⏰ Time - Fullscreen Digital, Analog & Word Clock App (`v2.0.0`)

[![GitHub Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/apravint/time-android?color=magenta&label=Latest%20Release)](https://github.com/apravint/time-android/releases/tag/v2.0.0)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Android Min SDK](https://img.shields.io/badge/Android-7.0%2B%20(API%2024)-brightgreen)](https://developer.android.com)

**Time v2.0** is an ultimate ambient full-screen digital, analog, and word clock app for Android. It features 14 custom Google Fonts, 10 vibrant neon themes, background starfield particle effects, synthesized audio ticks, custom size & glow adjusters, screen keep-on wake lock, and low-brightness night dimming.

Developed by **Ayyappa Pravin** (<apravint@gmail.com>).

---

## ⚡ What's New in v2.0.0

- 🔤 **14 Top-Notch Google Fonts**:
  - `Orbitron`, `Audiowide`, `Bungee 3D`, `Cyber Terminal (Share Tech Mono)`, `Outfit`, `JetBrains Mono`, `Syne Bold`, `Monoton`, `Comfortaa`, `Abril Fatface`, `Space Grotesk`, `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`, and `8-Bit Arcade 2P`.
- 🎨 **10 Dynamic Color Themes & Glow Palettes**:
  - **Cyber Neon**, **Sunset Cyberpunk**, **Vaporwave 80s**, **Tokyo Night**, **Aurora Borealis**, **OLED Stealth**, **Luxury Gold**, **Emerald Matrix**, **Crimson Red**, and **Nord Frost**.
- 🕒 **3 Clock Display Modes**:
  1. **Digital Clock**: High-precision giant digital clock digits.
  2. **Glowing Analog Clock**: Animated clock face with glowing hour, minute, and second hands.
  3. **Minimalist Word Clock**: Natural English text time display (e.g. `"IT IS SEVEN TWENTY-EIGHT PM"`).
- 🌌 **Starfield Particle Engine**: Dynamic background canvas particle animation.
- 🎚️ **Custom Sliders**: Text scale slider and neon glow intensity adjuster.
- 🔊 **Synthesized Soft Tick Sound**: Optional web audio synthesized clock ticking sound.
- 💡 **Wake-Lock & Night Mode**: Keep screen permanently awake and reduce brightness to protect OLED displays while sleeping.
