# Privacy Policy for ChronoCraft (Time App)

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

ChronoCraft ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure when you use the **ChronoCraft: Minimal Clock & Timer** mobile application for Android.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection Guarantee
ChronoCraft is built as an offline-first, distraction-free utility. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or usage metrics.**
* **No Account Required**: You do not need to register or sign in to use ChronoCraft.
* **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
* **No Location Data**: We do not access GPS or coarse/fine device location.
* **No Device Identifiers**: We do not collect Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IMEI, or MAC addresses.
* **No Analytics or Telemetry**: No tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK) are embedded in the application.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
ChronoCraft only requests essential system permissions required for local horology functions:
* **WAKE_LOCK**: Used strictly to keep the screen on while operating in Desk Clock or Nightstand Mode. You can disable this by turning off the display.
* **VIBRATE**: Used strictly to deliver local haptic feedback during timer completion or button presses.
* **No Internet Access**: ChronoCraft does not require or utilize network connectivity. It functions 100% offline.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
ChronoCraft contains zero third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or cloud synchronization services.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
ChronoCraft does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
If we make any modifications to this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
