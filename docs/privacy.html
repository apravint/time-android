ChronoCraft Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Guarantee

ChronoCraft is built as an offline-first, distraction-free utility. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or usage metrics.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register or sign in.
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • No Location Data: We do not access GPS or device location.
  • No Telemetry/Analytics: Zero third-party analytics or tracker SDKs.

2. Permissions Used

  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps screen awake while in Desk Clock or Nightstand Mode.
  • VIBRATE: Triggers local haptic feedback upon timer completion.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ChronoCraft does not collect data from any user, including children under 13. It complies fully with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies.
